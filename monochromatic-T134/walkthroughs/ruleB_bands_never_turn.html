Rule B: the bands never turn

Suppose rows 0, 1, 2 of the base lattice Λ have band offsets 0, 4, 1 — a step of 4 followed by 5. The rotated lattice qΛ, q = (3+5ω)/7 = π/π̄ with π = 2+ω, shares every seventh point with Λ. Whichever of its three directions is long — and after first using as much of Λ alone as possible —, two rules give a contradiction in a few forced steps, with no case splits: a forbidden triangle with two vertices of one colour forces the third, and points of qΛ four apart along its long direction have opposite colours.
